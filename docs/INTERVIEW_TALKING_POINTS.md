# Interview talking points for ThinkForge

Every number below comes from the repository documents. Use only quotes from participants who consented (P1, P3, P4, P6, P7, P9, P10).

## 30-second version

"I worked from 10 PM and APM interview records. All ten had a decision where a source or assumption was hard to find, and none had documented whether the outcome matched their prediction. I built a five-step decision record around that gap, cut everything the interviews didn't support, and wrote a paired pilot to test whether it beats a plain template. I haven't run the pilot yet, so I don't claim it improves decisions."

## Likely questions

**Why this problem?** 10/10 had fragmented or hidden reasoning and 10/10 had no documented outcome comparison. It held across company stage, seniority and decision type.

**What did you cut?** RAG tuning, benchmark tooling and Jira export. Reach was 2–4 of 10 at best, none were requested, two participants raised privacy unprompted, and external writes conflict with the advisory-only boundary all ten stated.

**How did you prioritize?** RICE, with reach taken from the interview counts and the other inputs labeled as my judgment. I would re-score after the pilot.

**What surprised you?** P7 called review process overkill for a toggle, which is why the pilot tests a lightweight path against a plain template instead of assuming a fuller workflow wins. Also that 5/10 described giving stakeholders higher confidence than they held privately.

**What are the limits of the research?** Ten mixed-recruitment records, notes supplied by me and not independently verified, a prompted AI question, and no coverage of willingness to pay. Descriptive counts only.

**What if the pilot fails?** The decision rules are written in advance: continue, narrow to a template, or stop. A template result is a legitimate outcome.

**What would you do differently?** _Fill this in yourself from your real timeline. If you built the technical layers before the interviews, say so and name what you would reorder; do not use this answer unless it is true._

**Did it improve decisions?** Not measured yet. Say that plainly; it is the strongest thing you can say about rigor.

## Numbers to keep straight

| Claim | Number | Note |

| Overstated confidence | 5/10 | Descriptive, not a general claim |

# ThinkForge interview guide

**Purpose:** Test the decision-recording problem, not pitch the product. Do not claim results until notes are collected and consent is documented.

## Recruitment and consent

Recruit 8–10 APMs/PMs. Ask permission to take anonymized notes. Do not collect employer secrets, customer PII, or sensitive roadmap details.

## 30-minute script

1. Tell me about the last meaningful product decision you owned.
2. What information did you use? Where did it live?
3. Which assumptions did you make? How did you record them, if at all?
4. How did you handle disagreement or missing evidence?
5. Could you now explain why the decision was made? What would be difficult to reconstruct?
6. Did you test the decision? What happened afterward?
7. What was the most time-consuming part of the process?
8. Show the five-step loop only after the prior questions. What would you use, ignore, or worry about?
9. Would a template solve this, or would a workspace add meaningful value? Why?
10. Who would need to approve such a workflow, and what data would they refuse to share?

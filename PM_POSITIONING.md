# ThinkForge positioning

| Alternative | Good for | Gap for this job | ThinkForge hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT alone | Drafting and brainstorming | No durable evidence links, decision record, or outcome log | Evidence-gated workflow makes reasoning easier to inspect later. |
| Notion / Docs | Flexible storage | Does not actively connect assumptions, evidence, experiment, and outcome | One loop reduces reconstruction work. |
| Productboard / Aha! | Roadmaps and feedback management | Optimized for portfolio planning, not one defensible decision | ThinkForge focuses on a single decision's reasoning trail. |

This is a positioning hypothesis, not a claim of market superiority. Interviews and pilot feedback determine whether it is true.

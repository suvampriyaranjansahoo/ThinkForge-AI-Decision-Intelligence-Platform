## Coding rules

- A participant counts once per theme, even if they mentioned it more than once.
- “Formal outcome review missing” means the participant described no documented comparison between an original prediction/target and an observed result. It does not mean no result existed.
- “Evidence fragmentation” means at least one material input was lost, unshared, personal, buried, or had unclear access/retention.
- Counts are descriptive for this sample only. They are not market estimates and should not be used with significance claims.

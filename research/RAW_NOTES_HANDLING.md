# Raw interview notes handling

The full interview notes were supplied to the project owner in a private workspace. They are not duplicated in this repository because they may contain participant context, employer details, and consent-sensitive material.

Before publishing the repository, store the raw notes in an access-controlled location and maintain a source register with participant ID, date, recruitment source, consent status, and file location. `INTERVIEW_EVIDENCE_LOG_2026_Q3.md` is the publishable metadata and coding record. It does not replace the raw notes.

Only use direct quotes from P1, P3, P4, P6, P7, P9, and P10 unless consent changes. Do not publish names, employer names, customer names, or private links.

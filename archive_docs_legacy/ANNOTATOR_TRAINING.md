# ThinkForge Annotator Training

## Stage 1 — Read
Review the rubric, evidence-label definitions, disagreement taxonomy, and study instructions.

## Stage 2 — Pilot
Complete 30 pilot cases independently. Record a 1–5 confidence value for each completed annotation.

## Stage 3 — Review
Study-level review checks completion, pilot quality, and hidden-duplicate consistency. Only certified raters proceed.

## Stage 4 — Main study
Work on the frozen candidate snapshot. Do not discuss cases with another rater. Do not infer quality from model self-scores.

## Escalation
When evidence interpretation or rubric ambiguity prevents a stable score, record the disagreement reason. The adjudicator resolves the final label without overwriting the original ratings.

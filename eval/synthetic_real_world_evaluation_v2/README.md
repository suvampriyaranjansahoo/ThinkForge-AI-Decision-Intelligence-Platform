# Synthetic Real-World Benchmark v2

150 real-world-source-grounded cases × 60 synthetic participant personas × 4 paired repeats × 2 conditions.

Paired design; bootstrap 95% CI; paired sign-flip sensitivity; difficulty and experience stratification.

All participants and observations are synthetic/model-generated. This is NOT human-subject evidence and
must not be described as causal evidence from real users.

# ThinkForge paired pilot protocol

**Status:** protocol only; no participant or quality result exists yet.

## Question

Does the five-step ThinkForge workflow help PMs produce a more traceable decision record than their usual process?

## Design

- Recruit 10 consenting PM/APM participants.
- Each participant completes one comparable decision using their normal workflow and one using ThinkForge; randomize order where practical.
- Two independent raters, blinded to condition, score exported decision briefs on a pre-agreed 1–5 rubric: assumptions surfaced, evidence traceability, trade-off clarity, experiment quality, and outcome plan.
- Capture time-to-completion and participant confidence as self-reports; these are not objective quality measures.

## Data record

For each participant and task record: anonymized ID, condition order, task, completion time, decision brief, linked-evidence count, outcome-plan present, self-reported confidence, rater scores, rater disagreement, and exclusions.

## Analysis and limits

Report raw paired rows, median/mean difference, sample size, missing data, and a confidence interval only if computed from the actual rows. Do not call the study causal, representative, or statistically conclusive with ten people.

## Stop conditions

Stop if a participant reveals confidential information, cannot consent, or the tool creates an unreviewed recommendation. Remove identifying information before publishing results.
